What is Arfa?

We’re Rafael and Adelya. Two people who love figuring out complex things and creating something new.

Today, we’re building devices with Raspberry Pi. Tomorrow, we’re launching a landing page in three days. In between, we work out product economics, write research papers, develop apps, and help others bring their ideas to life.

Arfa is our projects, research, and collaboration with people who want to build something of their own.

What We Do

Over time, we’ve built quite a few projects of our own. We’ve brought them together in one place so you can explore what we come up with, how we work, and what we create.

Most of our projects are available for free — explore, experiment, and get inspired.
